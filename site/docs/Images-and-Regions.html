Images and regions

All of this starts in the free cursor (Ctrl-v): a thin cross that can go anywhere on the page. h j k l move it; w b J K (or Ctrl-h/j/k/l) move it faster; v starts a rectangle.

Copying a region as an image#

For slides, a report, a message: mark a rectangle with v, then

KeyAction
ycopy it to the clipboard as a PNG; paste it anywhere
Ysave it as a PNG next to the PDF (paper-p5-1.png, paper-p5-2.png, …)

It is drawn at copy_dpi (300 dpi by default), in the page's own colours (also in dark mode), with highlights and filled-in forms as they are. The message says its size and resolution.

Putting a picture on a page#

A signature, a screenshot, a figure, a stamp:

HowAction
I in the free cursorchoose an image file (PNG, JPEG, BMP; Tab completes the path)
P in the free cursorpaste an image from the clipboard
:image file.pngfrom anywhere
  • With a rectangle marked (v), the picture fits inside it.
  • Otherwise its top-left corner goes at the cross, at its natural size, but never larger than 80% of the page.
  • Transparent PNGs stay transparent.
  • Right after it is placed, it can be moved and resized (below).

Pictures are standard stamp annotations: other readers show them, the panel lists them, x deletes them and u undoes.

Quality. Pictures stay sharp when you zoom in. Small ones (a signature, a screenshot) are stored with more pixels than the screen shows, enlarged with a bicubic filter, so they stay smooth up to about 250% zoom on a HiDPI screen. Images up to 4096 pixels are kept exactly (a JPEG is copied byte for byte); only very large ones are reduced, never below 4096 pixels on the longer side. A 24-megapixel photo goes in in under a second and adds about 2 MB to the file. Pictures over 120 megapixels are refused.

Moving, resizing, turning#

Put the cross on a picture or a highlighted area and press e:

KeyAction
h j k l (with Ctrl: faster)move
+ / -bigger / smaller, keeping the proportions
H / Lnarrower / wider
K / Jshorter / taller
=a picture back to its own proportions
r / Rturn a picture 90° clockwise / counter-clockwise
ccrop a picture (below)
xdelete it
Enterdone
Esccancel: back where it was

Highlighted text stays with its words; only areas and pictures move.

Cropping#

c while moving a picture:

KeyAction
h j k lcut the left / bottom / top / right side, as you see it (Ctrl cuts faster)
H J K Lgive that side back
uthe whole picture again
Enterback to moving and resizing
Esccancel this cropping

The picture itself is never cut or re-encoded: the PDF keeps it whole and only shows a part of it. So a crop, or a turn, can be undone at any time, even after the file was closed and opened again, with no loss of quality.
